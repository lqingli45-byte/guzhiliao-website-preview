About

Put AI into real life.

Gu Zhi Liao aims to combine AI with real-life situations and make simple digital tools people can actually use. It is a brand that can keep launching different tools, not a children’s education company.

What the brand is

谷知了, written in English as Gu Zhi Liao, is an independent digital-product brand. We pay attention to problems that keep coming up in daily life and work, and that are worth turning into tools: shortening preparation, making vague information clearer, and reducing unhelpful filtering and follow-up.

The first product in development is Read-Aloud Activity Workshop（共读活动工坊）, a read-aloud planning tool for parents. That only means we are starting from a concrete everyday situation. It does not mean Gu Zhi Liao only makes parenting or children’s education products, or only serves that kind of need. Other practical digital tools will follow.

How we work

A tool should make clear what problem it addresses, who it is for, and how far along it is. A prototype demo is labeled as a prototype demo. A product that cannot yet be purchased is not described as launched. Simulated responses are not described as live AI. We do not introduce products with exaggerated claims about results.

The name

The Chinese name is 谷知了. The English name is Gu Zhi Liao. It is a personal brand name, with a simple idea: useful knowledge should be ready to use when it is needed.
