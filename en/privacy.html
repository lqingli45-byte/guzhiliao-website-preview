Privacy policy

This notice covers this preview site, its online trial and the offline tool.

Trial and offline tool

The tool is intended for adult caregivers. It requires no registration and asks for no child’s name, date of birth, photo or other identifying information. Story, age range, time and reaction choices are processed on the device. The tool sends no activity data to Gu Zhi Liao or a model provider.

Local storage and deletion

The tool can save plans in the current browser or preview. It can also store a language preference locally. Delete individual plans in the saved list or clear site or file browser data to remove local records. Delete PDFs you export from your device separately.

Hosting and support email

GitHub Pages hosts the preview and may process connection information such as IP addresses. This preview uses no analytics tracking scripts or forms. If you email us, we use what you share to reply and handle your request. Please leave out children’s personal information.

Payment and digital delivery

This preview does not take payments. After checkout is enabled, Dodo Payments is expected to process payments and deliver download links. Applicable data handling details will be confirmed and available before checkout opens.
