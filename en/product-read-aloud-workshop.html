Read-Aloud Activity Workshop Lite v1.0

Pick a short story and get a line you can say out loud. If your child doesn’t feel like answering, switch to an action, let them watch, or stop for the day. For caregivers reading with children ages 4–6.

Who it’s for

For parents and caregivers of children aged four to six who want something simple to use at story time. An adult guides the activity and can stop whenever the child wants to.

Know what to say next

Open one step at a time for a suggestion tied to the story. Go back, skip a step or finish early.

Change course when talking stalls

Choose what happened and try a different kind of interaction. If it still isn’t working, take a break.

Adjust, undo and save on this device

Adjust the current step or undo the change. Save or delete plans on this device and print in a supported browser.

How it works

  1. Pick a story, age 4, 5 or 6, and five or ten minutes.
  2. Read along and use a line that feels natural to you.
  3. Change course, skip or finish as needed. Save the plan if you want to revisit it.

Try this feedback path

Open “How Can We Get the Drawing There?” in the trial and move to the interaction step. Under “How did your child respond?”, choose “That didn’t work” to try pointing. Choose “Still not working” to show the action yourself and let your child watch. If that still doesn’t work, pause or finish.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add my own story?

Lite works with the built-in stories. It selects written activities and responses based on the age and choices you make.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

The online trial needs a connection to load. After you download and extract the full edition, it works offline without an account, AI subscription, API key or model calls.

Where are saved plans stored?

Plans stay in the browser or preview where you saved them. Delete them in the saved list or clear browser site data. They do not sync to other devices.

What if I have trouble opening it?

Try the free edition in your browser first. For issues with the full download, send your device and opening method to lqingli45@gmail.com.
